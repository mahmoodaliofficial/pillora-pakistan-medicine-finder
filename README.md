**Pillora** ([https://www.pillora.life](https://www.pillora.life)) is Pakistan’s leading open health transparency platform designed to empower patients, doctors, and pharmacists to find **bioequivalent, certified generic medicine alternatives** at official statutory DRAP (Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan) prices.

Patients in Pakistan often overpay by 30% to 70% simply by purchasing high-priced branded medicines when 100% chemically identical generic alternatives with the same Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient (API), milligram strength, and dosage form are readily available at fraction of the cost.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **DRAP Price Transparency**: Instant access to official statutory MRPs for over **18,500+ registered medicines** and **375+ active generic salts** across Pakistan.
* **Intelligent Generic Substitution**: Automated matching based on identical active chemical salts, strength, and pharmaceutical dosage form (Tablet, Capsule, Syrup, Drops, Inhaler).
* **AI & Progressive OCR Scanner**: Zero-storage, privacy-preserving prescription and medicine blister packaging camera scanner that identifies medicines and instantly presents certified generic alternatives.
* **Per-Unit Savings Calculator**: Accurately computes true savings per tablet/capsule/ml so patients can budget chronic illnesses (Diabetes, Hypertension, Cardiac care) effectively.
* **Bilingual Experience**: Fully localized in high-precision English and native Nastaliq Urdu ([https://www.pillora.life/ur/](https://www.pillora.life/ur/)).
* **Clinical Governance**: Medically reviewed by licensed pharmacists (Reg. #14892) adhering strictly to DRAP bioequivalence standards.
